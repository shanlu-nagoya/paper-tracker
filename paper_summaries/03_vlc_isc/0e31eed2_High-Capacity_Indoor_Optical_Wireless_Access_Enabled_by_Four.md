## Abstract
In this letter, we propose a dual-polarization carrier-extracted direct detection (DP-CEDD) for high-speed indoor optical wireless communication. Benefiting from the polarization stability in free-space environment, active polarization tracking for co-transmitted optical carrier can be eliminated in the DP-CEDD, naturally suitable for optical wireless communication. With the capability of retrieving the complex-valued double-sideband (DSB) signals, the DP-CEDD can realize four-dimensional signal transmission. The system achieves a 448-Gb/s gross (355.1-Gb/s net) data rate in 9-m indoor free-space link without expensive local oscillators while meeting the eye-safety standard.
