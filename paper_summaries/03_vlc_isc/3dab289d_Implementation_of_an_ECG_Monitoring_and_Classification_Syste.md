## Abstract
This article presents a cardiac event-monitoring system that can support Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT) technology. The focus is on electrocardiogram (ECG) monitoring by wireless body sensor networks that enable the classification of heart arrhythmias. Our work is based on the concept of low-power wireless devices, where we implement actual hardware and a system with low complexity and high accuracy for monitoring heart disease. The widely used common features with a window size of 50 and an ensemble bagged tree algorithm as the best accurate classifier are applied to classify different types of ECG signals with an accuracy of 98.99%. The fine K-nearest-neighbor (KNN) classifier with low complexity and an accuracy of 97.78% is also suitable for classification in terms of real implementation. To the best of our knowledge, we discovered that the sampling rate of 45–100 Hz and the packet loss of less than 40.77% from wireless transmission do not much affect the ECG classification accuracy. Experiments in various scenarios using the XBee3 micro module and ESP32-C6 with Zigbee and Bluetooth communications show that the proposed system can efficiently work in real-world monitoring and ECG classification applications.
